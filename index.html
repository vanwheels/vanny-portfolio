On the audio side, I've composed, sound-designed, and directed voice-over for other teams' games. In between, I ship small dev kits and templates to help fund it all.

About

Hi, I'm Vanny

I work at the intersection of design and sound — building the systems a game runs on, then building the audio that makes them feel real. Most solo devs pick one side of that; I'd rather do both properly than outsource one and half-know it.

The dev kits and templates on this site aren't the main event — they're what keeps the lights on while Pantheon Tactics and ChoiceBuds get the time they actually need.
