About

Hi, I'm Vanny

I'm an indie developer splitting my time between two things: building Pantheon Tactics, a turn-based ARPG roguelike woven from world mythology, and ChoiceBuds, a team-building tool for competitive Pokémon VGC players.

Alongside those, I build and ship small, self-contained dev kits and templates — Tailwind component kits, Notion templates — fast, cheap products that help fund the bigger, slower-moving games.
