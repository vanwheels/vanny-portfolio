What it does

Built for theorycrafting and squad planning

Live, tooltip-accurate math

A full build editor for traits, skills, and gear, with damage, healing, and boon numbers computed live as you make changes — sourced from real GW2 wiki data, not static tooltip text.

Gear optimizer

Set stat floors and pick what to maximize, and it searches the full legal gear, rune, and infusion combo pool for the best fit against your target.

Chat-link import & export

Paste a build code copied in-game and it decodes straight into the editor — or copy one back out to share in Guild Wars 2 itself.

Squad composition planning

Lay out up to 10 parties of 5 with saved builds, or drop in lightweight placeholder picks for roles you haven't planned out yet.

Also includes

  • One-click screenshot export for sharing a build or squad comp
  • A companion Discord bot for running squad boards directly in a server
  • Auto-updates with in-app patch notes — no manual reinstalls
